Getting started

Are the lessons really free?

Yes. Every lesson on this site is free to use. There's no sign-up and no payment. Small ads on the site help keep it free.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Just open a lesson and start. Nothing to download and no login.

What level are the lessons for?

Beginner to pre-intermediate learners. Not sure of your level? Take the free 1-minute level check.

Where should I start?

Go to Start Here, take the level check, and follow the next step it gives you.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The site and lessons work on phones, tablets and computers in any modern browser.

About the lessons

How are these lessons different?

Every lesson teaches the rule first, then lets you practise — and every answer explains why it's right or wrong. The lessons were built and tested in real classrooms.

Who makes the lessons?

A TEFL-certified English teacher who teaches beginner to pre-intermediate learners in Cambodia. The site is an Online Business Awareness project.

Will more lessons be added?

Yes. There are already 14 free lessons in the lesson library, and more are on the way.

Teachers & parents

I'm a teacher. Can I use the lessons in my class?

Yes — you're welcome to open the lessons on your classroom screen and teach with them. Please link to this site rather than copying, selling or re-publishing the lessons.

How can I help my child use the site?

Sit together, read the examples out loud, and let your child tap the answers. Short sessions of 10–15 minutes a day work best.

Courses & the site

Is there a full course?

Yes. Learn English Like a Local: UK Culture, Accents & Real-Life English is on Udemy. Free places are sometimes available — see what's free right now.

Why are there ads?

The ads pay for the website so the lessons can stay free for everyone.

Still not sure? Just start.

The quickest way to understand the site is to try it. The level check takes one minute and tells you exactly where to begin.
