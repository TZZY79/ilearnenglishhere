How every lesson works

Every lesson follows the same simple pattern, so you always know what to do next:

  • Learn it — the rule or formula comes first, with clear examples.
  • Try it — quizzes, fill-the-gap and vote activities.
  • Understand it — every answer tells you why.
  • Use it — speaking and real-life practice to make it stick.
