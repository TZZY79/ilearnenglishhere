Warm-Up

Three Voices, One Sentence

10 mins
👥
Your Group TaskOne person in your group reads the sentence below three times — each time in a different voice. Everyone else listens closely and decides what is changing.
I love English.

Read it three ways — take turns!

The reader says the same sentence three times in three completely different voices. After all three, the group discusses: what changed?

🤖
Voice 1

Robot — flat, no feeling, like a machine reading.

😊
Voice 2

Happy — warm, gentle, a smile in the sound.

🤩
Voice 3

Excited — energy, speed, pure enthusiasm!

One person reads — the group listens silently.
After all three voices, discuss: what changed between each reading?
Now swap — another person in the group tries all three voices.

🙋 Check Your Understanding

Did the reader say the same words each time?

Did all three versions sound the same?

Which version is easiest to listen to?

Activity 1

Punctuation is Traffic Lights

15 mins
👥
Your Group TaskFirst, read the example sentences as a group — one wrong, one right. Then take turns reading the practice sentences aloud. The group claps every time someone pauses correctly!

Full Stop (.)

STOP. Take a breath. A new idea begins.

Comma (,)

Small pause. The sentence isn't finished. Keep going.

No punctuation

Keep reading. Flow forward. Don't stop.

Hear the difference — read both aloud as a group

🚫 Wrong (no pause): "I woke up early I went to school" — rushed, hard to follow.

✅ Correct (with pause): "I woke up early.    I went to school." — clear, natural, easy to understand.

Activity 4

Read · Think · Explain

15 mins
📖
Your Group TaskRead the story together. Answer the questions as a group. Then close the screen — can you retell the story to each other from memory?
Jack was late for school. He ran down the street as fast as he could. When he arrived, the teacher smiled and said, "You are just in time." Jack felt relieved.

📝 Answer the Questions — discuss first, then write

1. Who is the story about?

2. Why was Jack running?

3. How did Jack feel at the end? Why?

4. What does "relieved" mean? Can you think of a time you felt relieved?
