🎯 How to playTap a letter (or use your keyboard). Right letters appear in the word. Each wrong letter adds a part to the drawing — 6 wrong letters and the round is over.
🧠 Smart tipsStart with vowels: E, A, O, I. Then try common letters: T, N, S, R, L. Use the topic to guess the word family.
🗣️ After every wordRead the meaning, press 🔊 to hear it, then say your own sentence with the word. Teachers: play in teams on the board!
