Lesson 3: Future Continuous

We use this for actions that will be in progress at a specific time in the future.
Subject + will + be + [Verb]-ing

Example: "Tomorrow at 6 AM, I will be running in the park."

Future Continuous: Practice

Read these out loud to practice the rhythm:
Teacher: "What will you be doing on Sunday?"
Student: "I will be resting and flossing my teeth!"

Lesson 4: Fitness Tips (It/That Clauses)

"It" is an empty subject. We use it to give general advice or tips.
It is [Adjective] + that + [Subject] + [Verb]

Example: "It is important that you drink water every hour."

Grammar: Intensifiers

Use these to show the strength of your pain or feelings.
Quite < Very < So < Extremely

Example: "My stomach is extremely painful!" (High pain)

Unit 4: Present Perfect (Appointments)

Use this for life experiences when we don't say exactly when.
Subject + have/has + V3 (Past Participle)

Example: "I have made an appointment for Tuesday."

Symptoms & Illnesses

Common words for Lesson 2:
  • Headache / Fever: Feeling hot and head pain.
  • Cough / Sore Throat: Pain when talking/breathing.
  • Nausea: Feeling like you might vomit.

Present Perfect vs. Simple Past

This is a major exam topic!
Simple Past: Specific time (Yesterday, 2022).
Present Perfect: No specific time (Ever, Never).

Example: "I visited him yesterday." vs "I have visited him before."

Roleplay: At the Doctor

Read this out loud with a partner:
Doctor: "What's the matter?"
Patient: "I've had a fever since yesterday. My head is extremely painful."
Doctor: "Have you taken any medicine?"

Grammar: Too and Enough

Describing if something is "more than needed" or "the right amount."
Too + Adjective / Adjective + Enough

Example: "This brush is too hard." / "My teeth are clean enough."

Grammar: Want / Would Like

Used to give orders or make polite requests.
Subject + would like + Object + To + Verb

Example: "I would like you to floss every night."

Classroom Game: The Body Race

Non-Digital Game Instructions:

1. Split into two teams. 2. Teacher calls out a body part. 3. First student to touch that part on themselves and say "My [Part] is very painful!" wins a point for their team.

Exam Success Tips

How to remember these topics:
  • Future Cont: Look for "tomorrow at [Time]".
  • Perfect vs Past: If there is a date, use Simple Past.
  • Too/Enough: "Too" comes Before. "Enough" comes After.
