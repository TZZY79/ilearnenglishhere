5 Physical Classroom Games  ·  No Devices  ·  Max 1 Hour Each

🏃
Visiting Relatives · Future Tense
🏃 Future Relay Race
⏱ 45 mins 👥 Whole class — teams of 4–5
🎯 Goal: Be the first team to make 5 correct future tense sentences about visiting relatives!
📦 What You Need
  • Word cards (use the Print Cards section)
  • Blu-tack or tape to stick cards on the board
  • A wall or board
  • Paper & pen per team
🔧 Setup — Teacher Does This First
1
Stick all word cards on the board — mix them up. There are 4 types: Subjects (I, She, We...), Verbs (will visit, will bring...), Objects (my aunt, some gifts...), and Time Words (tonight, next week...).
2
Divide the class into teams of 4–5. Teams stand in a line, far from the board.
3
Give each team a piece of paper and a pen.
🕹️ How to Play
1
The first player runs to the board and takes ONE word card.
2
They run back and give the card to their team.
3
The next player runs and takes another card. Keep going!
4
When your team has enough words, build a sentence and write it on your paper. Example: "She will visit her cousins tonight."
5
Show the teacher. ✅ Correct → keep the cards and start a new sentence. ❌ Wrong → put the cards back on the board.
6
First team with 5 correct sentences WINS! 🏆
💡 Teacher Tip: After the game, ask each team to read their sentences aloud. Correct any mistakes together as a class.

Blind Date · Dating Phrases · Predictions
💘 Blind Date Bingo
⏱ 50 mins 👥 Whole class
🎯 Goal: Find a classmate for each square on your Bingo card. Get 5 in a row to win!
📦 What You Need
  • 1 Bingo card per student — printed from the Print Cards section
  • Pens or pencils
  • Dating phrases list written on the board
🔧 Setup — Teacher Does This First
1
Print one Bingo card per student from the Print Cards section. Each card has squares with predictions like "Will probably be funny" or "Might like action movies".
2
Give each student one card and a pen.
3
Write these dating phrases on the board: "What are your hobbies?" / "Do you think you will...?" / "I think you might..." / "Will you...?"
🕹️ How to Play
1
Stand up and walk around the classroom.
2
Find a partner. Introduce yourselves using the phrases on the board.
3
If a square on your card matches your partner (e.g. they ARE probably funny), write their name in that square.
4
One name per square. You cannot use the same person twice. Then find a new partner!
5
Get 5 names in a row (across, down, or diagonal) → shout "BINGO! 💘"
6
Read your winning row aloud to the class using full sentences: "Omar will probably be funny. He might like..."
7
Teacher checks sentences. Correct = YOU WIN! 🎉
💡 Teacher Tip: Play two rounds. Round 1 = find your Bingo. Round 2 = share the funniest or most surprising prediction with the class!

Going to the Movies · Movie Types · Modals of Possibility
🎬 Movie Mime Madness
⏱ 40 mins 👥 Whole class — teams of 3–4
🎯 Goal: Act out movie scenes! Other teams guess using "might", "could", and "must be".
📦 What You Need
  • Movie Genre Cards + Scene Cards — printed and folded
  • A hat or bag to draw cards from
  • Score chart on the board
🔧 Setup — Teacher Does This First
1
Print, cut, and fold the Genre Cards and Scene Cards from the Print Cards section. Put them all in a hat.
2
Divide the class into teams of 3–4. Draw a score chart on the board with team names.
3
Write the guessing rules on the board: "It might be... / It could be... / It must be..."
🕹️ How to Play
1
One player from Team A picks a card from the hat. They do NOT show anyone.
2
They have 30 seconds to act out the movie. NO talking. NO sounds.
3
The other teams guess — Team A's own members cannot guess!
4
Guesses must use a modal: "It could be a horror film!" / "It must be science fiction!"
5
A plain guess like "Horror!" gets NO point. Always use the modal!
6
First team to guess correctly with a modal verb gets 1 point.
7
Rotate — next team sends an actor. After all cards, most points WINS! 🎬
💡 Teacher Tip: Before you start, practice the modals together. Write on the board: "It might be... / It could be... / It must be..." — insist students use them every time. No modal = no point!

Nightlife · That Clause · A New Business
🌙 Nightlife Story Chain
⏱ 55 mins 👥 Groups of 5–6
🎯 Goal: Build a crazy nightlife story using "that" clauses — then pitch a new business idea!
📦 What You Need
  • Nightlife vocabulary cards — one set per group
  • A soft ball or rolled-up paper ball per group
  • Paper & pens for the pitch task
🔧 Setup — Teacher Does This First
1
Print and cut the nightlife vocabulary cards. Give one full set to each group.
2
Students sit in circles of 5–6. Each group gets one soft ball.
3
Write on the board: "I think that... / I heard that... / I know that... / We believe that..."
🕹️ How to Play
1
PART 1 — Story Chain (20 mins). The first player holds the ball and starts: "I think that we should go to a nightclub tonight."
2
They throw the ball to any other player. That player continues the story with "that": "I heard that the nightclub has live music!"
3
Every sentence MUST include "that". If someone cannot say a sentence in 5 seconds — they are OUT for this round.
4
Last person left in the circle wins the round! Play 2–3 rounds.
5
PART 2 — Business Pitch (25 mins). Groups invent a new nightlife business using their vocabulary cards.
6
Write 5 sentences about your business. Every sentence must use "that": "We believe that people want..."
7
Each group stands up and presents. The class votes for the best idea! 🌙
💡 Teacher Tip: For Part 2, give groups this template on the board: "We believe that... / We know that... / We think that... / Our idea is that... / We hope that..."
🗺️
Day Trips · Weekend Plans · Suggesting with Modal Verbs
🗺️ Weekend Trip Negotiation
⏱ 50 mins 👥 Groups of 4
🎯 Goal: Plan the PERFECT weekend trip — but your group must AGREE using modal verbs!
📦 What You Need
  • Destination cards, Budget cards, Problem cards (printed)
  • Large paper or poster board per group
  • Markers
🔧 Setup — Teacher Does This First
1
Print and cut the three card types: Destination (beach, mountain...), Budget ("You have $50 each"), and Problem cards (face down — reveal later!).
2
Divide into groups of 4. Each group draws 1 Destination card, 1 Budget card, and 1 Problem card (do NOT look at the Problem card yet).
3
Write the Modal Menu on the board: "Why don't we... / We could... / Shall we... / Let's... / We might... / How about..."
🕹️ How to Play
1
Look at your Destination and Budget. Plan your weekend trip! You have 10 minutes.
2
RULE: Every suggestion must use a modal verb. If someone forgets, the group says "Use a modal!" and they try again.
3
After 10 minutes, the teacher reveals the Problem card. You have 5 more minutes to solve it — still using modals!
4
Each group presents in 2 minutes: WHERE you are going, HOW you get there, WHAT you will do, and how you solved the PROBLEM.
5
Class votes: Which trip is most fun? Which team used the most modal verbs? 🗺️
💡 Teacher Tip: Keep the Problem card face-down until you say "Problem time!" — the surprise makes it more exciting and forces real-time negotiation.
