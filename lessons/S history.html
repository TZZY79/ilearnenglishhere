The 3 Plural Endings 📚
This is a rule — not random magic!
/s/
After voiceless sounds
cat → cats
book → books
map → maps
/z/
After voiced sounds
dog → dogs
pen → pens
bag → bags
/ɪz/
After s, sh, ch, x, z
bus → buses
watch → watches
box → boxes
💡 Same rule applies to 3rd person verbs: he runs, she eats, it watches

🛒 Another invisible "s"

French influence at work!
sugar
🍬 S + U becomes /ʃ/ (sh)
"SHUG-er" not "SUG-er"
⚠️ English is not 100% logical — history affects spelling.
Words borrowed from other languages kept their original spelling
but English speakers changed how they were pronounced over time.
