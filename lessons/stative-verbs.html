Stative Verbs with Continuous Meanings+ Collocations with Go, Do & Play

🎯 By the end of this lesson you can:
  • Use some stative verbs in continuous tenses ✅
  • Choose correctly between go, do & play ✅
  • Avoid the most common student mistakes ✅
🔥 Warm-Up · 5 min

Look & Think!

Read these two sentences. Talk to a partner:

Sentence A

"I think English is important."

What tense? Why?

Sentence B

"I am thinking about my future."

What tense? Why?

💬 Do both sentences use "think" the same way?

No! "Think" can be STATIVE (opinion) or ACTION (process). Same word — two different meanings! That's today's lesson. 🎉
📚 Quick Review

Remember: Action vs Stative

🎬 Action = Movie  |  📷 Stative = PhotoAction verbs move. Stative verbs stay still.
❌ I am knowing the answer.
✅ I know the answer.
But wait… today we learn some stative verbs CAN be used in continuous — when the meaning changes! 🤯
🔄 Dual-Meaning Verbs · THINK

THINK — Two Meanings!

Stative (Opinion)Subject + think + that + opinion
Action (Process/Activity)Subject + am/is/are thinking + about + object

📷 Opinion (Stative)

I think Angkor Wat is beautiful.

= my opinion, always true

🎬 Process (Action)

I am thinking about my trip to Siem Reap.

= I am doing it right now

💬 "She is thinking about her exam." — Is she having an opinion or is it a process happening now?

A process happening now! She is actively thinking. ✅ Continuous is correct here.
🔄 Dual-Meaning Verbs · HAVE

HAVE — Two Meanings!

Stative (Possession)I have a motorbike. (I own it)
Action (Activity / Experience)I am having lunch. / We are having a great time!

📷 Possession

She has a bicycle.

🎬 Activity

She is having a banh mi for lunch!

❌ I am having a new phone. (possession)
✅ I have a new phone.

💬 "We are having a party tonight!" — Possession or Activity?

Activity! 🎉 "Having a party" is an experience/event. Continuous is correct!
👁️ Sense Verbs · SEE / LOOK / WATCH

See, Look & Watch

SEE = stative

I see a temple. (automatic / happens by itself)

LOOK = action

I am looking at the temple. (choosing to do it)

WATCH = action

I am watching the monks. (paying attention)

👁️ See = it happens. 👀 Look / Watch = you choose.

💬 "I ___ a beautiful sunset over the Mekong." — see / am looking at / am watching?

All three can work! But meaning changes: SEE = it appeared in my view AM LOOKING AT = I chose to look AM WATCHING = I am paying attention to it moving
👃 Sense Verbs · TASTE / SMELL / FEEL

Taste, Smell & Feel

📷 Stative (describe quality)

This amok tastes delicious!

The market smells amazing.

The weather feels hot today.

🎬 Action (you are doing it)

I am tasting the soup. (checking it)

She is smelling the flowers. (choosing to)

He is feeling better now. (process)

💬 "The chef ___ the curry." — taste / is tasting?

IS TASTING ✅ — the chef is actively checking the flavour. It's an action!
📖 Reading · Cambodia Story

Read Together!

Sophea is thinking about her weekend. She has a motorbike and she usually goes to Kep beach.

Right now, she is having breakfast at a café in Phnom Penh. She can see the river from her table — the water looks beautiful this morning.

Her coffee smells amazing. She is tasting it slowly because it is very hot.

She thinks Saturday will be a perfect day for the beach. She knows the road well, so she is not worried.

💬 Why do we use "is thinking" but "thinks" in the same passage?

"Is thinking" = she is doing the mental process right now (active). "Thinks" = her opinion (stative). Same verb — different meaning!

💬 "She has a motorbike." Can we say "She is having a motorbike"?

No! ❌ "Have" = possession here. We cannot use continuous. ✅ "She has a motorbike."

Part 2

Collocations withGO · DO · PLAY

Which verb do we use with sports and activities? Let's find out!

⚽ PLAY

PLAY — Sport & Games

RuleUse PLAY with competitive sports & games — things with a ball or opponent 🏆
play footballplay basketballplay badmintonplay chessplay cardsplay volleyball

Every weekend, Dara plays football at the Olympic Stadium in Phnom Penh. After the game, his friends play cards in the park.

💬 Is there a winner and a loser in football? → Does "play" fit? Why?

Yes! Competitive sports with opponents → use PLAY ⚽
🏊 GO

GO — Activities ending in -ING

RuleUse GO with activities that end in -ing — usually done alone 🌊
go swimminggo runninggo cyclinggo hikinggo fishinggo shopping

On Sunday mornings, Maly goes running along the Tonle Sap river. Sometimes she goes cycling to the night market with her sister.

💬 Can you "go chess"? Why not?

No! ❌ Chess doesn't end in -ing as an activity. You PLAY chess. GO = solo activities with -ing. 🚴
🥋 DO

DO — Individual & Non-Team

RuleUse DO with martial arts, fitness, solo activities & tasks 💪
do karatedo yogado homeworkdo exercisedo aerobicsdo gymnastics

Bopha does yoga every morning before school. Her brother does karate at a gym near Wat Phnom. In the evening, they both do their homework together.

💬 Why "do yoga" and not "play yoga" or "go yoga"?

Yoga is not competitive (not PLAY) and it doesn't become "yogaing" (not GO). It's a solo fitness activity → DO! 🧘
📊 Summary · Go / Do / Play

The Big Table!

GO

Activities + -ing
Usually solo

  • 🏊 go swimming
  • 🚴 go cycling
  • 🏃 go running
  • 🎣 go fishing
  • 🛍️ go shopping

DO

Solo / fitness /
martial arts / tasks

  • 🥋 do karate
  • 🧘 do yoga
  • 💪 do exercise
  • 📚 do homework
  • 🤸 do gymnastics

Competitive sports
& games / team

  • ⚽ play football
  • 🏸 play badminton
  • 🎮 play video games
  • ♟️ play chess
  • 🏀 play basketball
🎯 Memory Trick
GO = "Go-ing" (the activity already has -ing!)  |  PLAY = you can win  |  DO = everything else
⚠️ Common Student Errors

Don't Make These Mistakes!

❌ I am thinking English is easy.
✅ I think English is easy. (opinion)
❌ I am having a bicycle.
✅ I have a bicycle. (possession)
❌ She does swimming every day.
✅ She goes swimming every day.
❌ He plays yoga in the morning.
✅ He does yoga in the morning.
❌ They go football after school.
✅ They play football after school.

Optional Classroom Games

Choose a game to play with your students!

Game 1 · 5–10 min · Whole class

🏃 Activity Race!

Write GO / DO / PLAY on the board (or three corners of the room). Teacher calls out an activity. Students run to the correct corner.

  • 📢 "Swimming!" → Students run to GO
  • 📢 "Karate!" → Students run to DO
  • 📢 "Football!" → Students run to PLAY

Last person to arrive is out. Last student standing wins! 🏆

Game 2 · 10 min · Pairs

🃏 Sentence Snap!

Print or write 10 sentences on cards (mix of correct and incorrect). Students take turns reading one. Partner shouts:

  • 👍 "Correct!" → keep the card
  • 👎 "Wrong!" → say the correct version to steal the card

Most cards at the end wins. Great for error correction!

Game 3 · 10 min · Teams

🔮 Cambodia Quiz Buzz!

Divide into 2 teams. Teacher reads a question about the lesson using Cambodia contexts. First team to buzz in and answer correctly gets a point.

  • ❓ "Dara is swimming in the Mekong. GO, DO or PLAY?" → GO
  • ❓ "Correct this: She is having a tuk-tuk." → She has a tuk-tuk.
  • ❓ "I ___ chess at the park every Sunday." → play
  • ❓ "Is 'I am thinking about Angkor Wat' correct?" → Yes! (process)
Game 4 · 5 min · Solo

🎯 Find Someone Who…

Students walk around the class asking questions using today's language:

  • Find someone who goes swimming → Ask: "Do you go swimming?"
  • Find someone who plays badminton
  • Find someone who does yoga or karate
  • Find someone who thinks Phnom Penh is a great city

Must write the person's name. First to fill all 4 wins! ✍️

🔄 Stative Verbs with Continuous Meanings

VerbStative MeaningContinuous Meaning
thinkI think it's good. (opinion)I am thinking about it. (process)
haveI have a bike. (possession)I am having lunch. (experience)
seeI see a temple. (automatic)I am seeing a doctor. (appointment)
tasteIt tastes great. (quality)I am tasting the soup. (action)
smellIt smells nice. (quality)She is smelling flowers. (action)
feelIt feels cold. (quality)I am feeling better. (process)

⚽ GO / DO / PLAY — Quick Rules

GO

Activity + -ing
go swimming
go running
go cycling

DO

Solo / fitness / tasks
do karate
do yoga
do homework

PLAY

Competitive / team
play football
play chess
play badminton

🧠 Memory Tricks

  • THINK: "I think = my brain's photo. I am thinking = my brain's movie." 📷🎬
  • HAVE: "Have a car = own it (static). Having a party = living it (action)." 🚗🎉
  • GO: "Can you say go ___-ing? Then use GO!" (go swimming ✅, go yoga ❌)
  • PLAY: "Is there a winner? Then PLAY." 🏆
  • DO: "Everything else solo or fitness." 💪

❌ Most Common Errors

  • I am thinking it's a good idea. → I think it's a good idea.
  • I am having a new bag. → I have a new bag.
  • She does swimming. → She goes swimming.
  • He plays karate. → He does karate.
  • They go football. → They play football.
