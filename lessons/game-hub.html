⚡ Phase 1 — The Relay

How it works

  1. Teams line up behind the starting line
  2. Runner 1 sprints to board, writes ONE unique word from: Summer Olympic sport, suggestion phrase, or gym body part
  3. Passes the marker, tags next runner
  4. Duplicates across teams are disqualified

Scoring

1 pt for every unique vocabulary word written

🔥 Phase 2 — The Match

How it works

  1. Flashcards from both columns are shuffled face-up on a table at the front
  2. Next runner finds ONE correct pair (e.g. LOSE + WEIGHT)
  3. Sticks it to team's section on board
  4. Must shout the definition to their team

Scoring

2 pts for every correct collocation + definition pair

🤒 PATIENT

Your Mission

Explain your bizarre or exaggerated problem! Be dramatic. The weirder, the better.

Scenario Ideas

  • Severe cat hair allergy making you sneeze at work
  • Pulled a muscle trying to lift too much at the gym
  • Extreme exam stress causing headaches
  • Terrible sleep from watching the Olympics all night
  • Sore neck from always looking down at phone

Language to Use

"I've developed a..." "Since last week I feel..." "It's getting worse because..." "I've been suffering from..."

🩺 THERAPIST

Your Mission

Listen carefully. Diagnose the problem. Give at least two recommendations.

Target Language

"You'd better..." "I recommend that you..." "How about...?" "Why don't you...?" "I suggest that you..." "It's important that you..." "You should avoid..." "Have you considered...?"

Checklist

  • ☐ Ask at least one follow-up question
  • ☐ Give at least 2 recommendations
  • ☐ Use a modal verb + that-clause structure

📖 Present Perfect — Quick Rules

Structure

Subject + have/has + past participle
I have never broken a bone.
She has never watched the Olympics live.
We have never been to a gym.

Common Mistakes to Catch

  • ❌ "I never go to the gym" — wrong tense
  • ✓ "I have never been to the gym"
  • ❌ "I have never loosed weight" — wrong form
  • ✓ "I have never lost weight"

Three-sentence rule — Past Perfect → Present Continuous → Future/Modal. Pass and twist.

Teams of 3–5 3 min per picture Clockwise rotation
WRITING TIMER

✍️ Three-Sentence Sentence Builder

Teams use this structure for every picture. Display on projector for reference.

S1
PAST PERFECT — What had happened BEFORE the photo?
The athlete had trained for six months before this moment.
S2
PRESENT CONTINUOUS — What IS happening RIGHT NOW?
She is crossing the finish line and is raising both arms.
S3
FUTURE / MODAL — What SHOULD or WILL happen next?
She will receive a medal and might break the world record.
