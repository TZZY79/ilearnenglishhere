How the Skills Lab works

  • 🔤 Letter rules — the rules of English spelling. ALWAYS no exceptions · USUALLY true for most words (we show the exceptions) · HINT helpful, but check.
  • 📖 Reading — did you read it, did you understand it, and can you put the paragraphs in the right order?
  • ✍️ Fix It — find the mistake, fix it, learn why people make it.
  • 🧩 Put It Together — write your own text. An automatic checklist checks the rules.
  • 🏆 Combo Challenge — 10 mixed questions, including some from earlier levels. 8 out of 10 opens the next level. Already strong? Take the Combo straight away.
  • Keep making the same grammar mistake? The site takes you to your Coach to fix it first.
