Task 2 · Reading✅ Done

📖 Read a property advert like a local

Adverts use short forms. 3-bed = three bedrooms · semi = semi-detached (joined to one other house) · GCH = gas central heating · en-suite = a bathroom attached to a bedroom · OIRO = offers in the region of · EPC = energy rating certificate.

TO LET / FOR SALE — WILLOW CLOSE 2-bed ground-floor flat · Leasehold (95 years remaining) OIRO £178,500 · Service charge £1,200 p.a. Open-plan living area, modern kitchen, bathroom with shower, GCH, allocated parking space. 5 mins walk to the high street. EPC: B. No pets permitted under the terms of the lease.
Task 3 · Formal writing✅ Done

✉️ Write a formal email to an estate agent

Situation: You saw the Willow Close flat (Task 2). Write to the agent, Ms Rana Aziz, to ask for a viewing. Include: a formal greeting, why you're writing, two times you're available, one question about the flat, and a formal ending. (60+ words)

Need help? Useful phrases

Dear Ms Aziz, · I am writing to enquire about… · I would be grateful if… · I am available on… · Could you please let me know whether… · I look forward to hearing from you. · Kind regards, / Yours sincerely,

    Task 4 · Numbers & money✅ Done

    💷 Prices, percentages and deposits

    In English we say £178,500 as "a hundred and seventy-eight thousand, five hundred pounds". A 10% deposit on £178,500 = £17,850. Type numbers only (no £ or commas needed).
