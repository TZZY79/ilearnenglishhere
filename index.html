Built by a teacher who sees the mistakes

This site was started by an English teacher who hears the same mistakes in every class — and saw that most English websites are made for people who already speak English. Here we focus on the part usual lessons skip: the common mistakes, breaking bad patterns, and understanding why, how and when — not just memorising the pattern.

“I have 20 years.” — a normal lesson just marks it wrong. Here you learn why your brain says it (your first language) and how to stop: “I am 20.”
